Ways to pay

Every invoice says how much is due and how to pay it.

Clients outside India

Card or bank transfer

Pay by card in US dollars, euros or pounds on Razorpay’s secure checkout. Enter the invoice number and amount from your invoice.

  • Visa, Mastercard and American Express
  • Pay in USD, EUR or GBP
  • Instant receipt by email

Prefer an international bank transfer (SWIFT)? Request bank details and they’ll come with your invoice.

Clients in India

UPI, card or net banking

Pay in rupees with any UPI app, an Indian debit or credit card, or net banking. Enter the invoice number and amount from your invoice.

  • Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps
  • Indian cards and net banking
  • Instant receipt by email

  1. IQuoteThe price, deposit and timeline for your project.
  2. IIInvoiceThe amount due and how to pay it.
  3. IIIPayBy bank transfer, UPI or card. Your receipt follows by email.
Which currency will I pay in?

US dollars, euros or pounds if you are outside India, or Indian rupees if you are in India, as set out in your invoice.

When do I pay?

A deposit before work starts and the balance before the full-resolution files are delivered, as set out in your quote.

Are there any fees?

None from me. Your card provider may add its own fee for paying in a foreign currency. For a bank transfer, please choose the option where you pay your bank’s charges (OUR), so the full invoice amount arrives.

Is there a limit on card payments?

Online card payments from outside India are limited to about US$450 each. For a larger invoice, I can split it into instalments or send bank transfer details.

Is it secure?

Payments are processed by Razorpay. I never see or store your card details or bank logins.
