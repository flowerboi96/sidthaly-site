Ways to pay

Every invoice says how much is due and how to pay it.

Clients outside India

Bank transfer

Pay from your own bank in US dollars, euros or pounds, the same way you would pay someone locally. From anywhere else, pay by SWIFT. The account details come with your invoice.

  • USD (ACH), EUR (SEPA), GBP (Faster Payments)
  • SWIFT from any other country
  • No card fees

Clients in India

UPI, card or net banking

Pay in rupees with any UPI app, an Indian debit or credit card, or net banking. Enter the invoice number and amount from your invoice.

  • Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps
  • Indian cards and net banking
  • Instant receipt by email

Which currency will I pay in?

US dollars, euros, pounds or Indian rupees, agreed in your quote.

When do I pay?

A deposit before work starts and the balance before the full-resolution files are delivered, as set out in your quote.

Are there any fees?

None from me. If you pay by SWIFT, please choose the option where you cover your own bank’s charges, so the full invoice amount arrives.

Can I pay by card from outside India?

Not yet. For now, clients outside India pay by bank transfer.

Is it secure?

Payments are handled by Razorpay. I never see or store your card or bank login details.
